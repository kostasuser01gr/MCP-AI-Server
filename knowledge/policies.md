### Seasons
- **Low**: November – March
- **Mid**: April – May, October

## Rental Rules
- Minimum rental: 1 day (24 hours)
- Maximum rental: 90 days
- Fuel policy: return at same level or pay €2/litre surcharge
- Late return: charged per extra hour (1/8 of daily rate), max 1 day

## Insurance
- Basic CDW included, excess €800
- Full CDW: +€12/day, excess €0
- Theft protection: +€8/day
- Personal accident: +€5/day

## Locations

- **Airport Desk**: Heraklion N. Kazantzakis, 06:00–23:00

## Cancellation
- Free cancellation up to 48h before pickup
- 50% charge within 48h
- No-show: full charge

## Damages
- All damage is photographed and documented at checkout and checkin
- Customer is liable for damage up to CDW excess
- Tyre/glass damage is not covered under basic CDW
